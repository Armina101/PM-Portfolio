01

The problem

It started with a weekend of matching bank alerts to names.

It started with a weekend I spent matching bank alerts to people for a friend's asoebi, the story I opened our pitch with.

When everyone in a group pays into one account, the collector has to work out who each alert belongs to, and who still hasn't paid.

02

How it works

Every payer gets their own account, so every payment arrives already labelled.

  1. Click to payThe moment a member clicks to pay, ALATPay's Bank Transfer API gives them a unique virtual account.
  2. Money landsWebhooks update both sides' dashboards the instant the transfer arrives.
  3. Collector sees it liveA live payment history and transaction monitoring, without opening a bank app.
03

Decisions and trade-offs

The goal was to remove the matching work entirely, not make it faster.

  1. Identify payers by account, not by alert

    What I chose
    A unique virtual account for every payer, created the moment they click to pay.
    Instead of
    One shared account, with alerts matched to names afterwards.
    What it cost
    An account to create and track for every payment.

  2. Real time on both sides

    What I chose
    Webhooks update the payer's and the collector's dashboards the instant money lands.
    Instead of
    Collectors checking their bank app and updating a list.
    What it cost
    More moving parts to build and monitor.
