Principles I build by.

Four things I believe about building products, and the moments that taught me each one.

01

Users · learned on Zuri

Look for the problem people have learned to live with.

Before Zuri, I thought calculating my balance before opening my banking app was just my habit. Then I spoke to friends, professionals and parents who did the same. What seemed personal was actually a shared problem.

See the Zuri project

02

Experience · learned on the hackathon platform

Design for everyone in the room.

On the hackathon platform, I designed for judges, participants and admins. Judges needed simple scoring, teams needed accessible private feedback, and admins needed a way to correct errors without changing a judge's comments.

See the hackathon project

03

Ownership · learned on StacStart × iDICE

Give the team the problem, then get out of the way.

During the iDICE rebuild, I set clear goals and priorities, made progress visible in Jira, and trusted engineers to own their solutions. Everyone knew what mattered without needing constant oversight.

See the iDICE project

04

Strategy · learned on iDICE and Mimuads

Decide what not to build yet.

On iDICE, we prioritised bulk onboarding before job-readiness tools. At Mimuads, we validated venue-host interest before building the booking engine. Both taught me that knowing what to delay matters as much as knowing what to build.

See the iDICE and Mimuads projects
